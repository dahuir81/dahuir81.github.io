关于建议分拆平头哥并于港交所主板独立上市的公告 Proposed Spin-off and Separate Listing of T-Head on the Main Board of The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited

阿里巴巴集团控股有限公司 · Alibaba Group Holding Limited（开曼群岛注册成立 · Incorporated in the Cayman Islands with limited liability）
股份代号 Stock Code：9988（港币柜台 HKD Counter）／89988（人民币柜台 RMB Counter）

一、核心事项1. Key Facts

阿里巴巴集团宣布，已根据上市规则《第 15 项应用指引》（PN15）就建议分拆平头哥（上海）技术股份有限公司（"平头哥"）并独立上市，向香港联交所提交建议。平头哥为公司全资子公司。

Alibaba Group Holding Limited announced that it has submitted a proposal in relation to the proposed spin-off and separate listing of T-Head (Shanghai) Technology Co., Ltd. (“T-Head”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company, to the Hong Kong Stock Exchange pursuant to PN15 of the Listing Rules.

公司欣然宣布，香港联交所已确认公司可进行建议分拆；为此，平头哥已于 2026 年 9 月 11 日以保密方式向香港联交所提交上市申请表格（A1 表格），申请平头哥 H 股于联交所主板上市及买卖。

The Company is pleased to announce that the Hong Kong Stock Exchange has confirmed that the Company may proceed with the Proposed Spin-off, and therefore, on September 11, 2026, T-Head, on a confidential basis, submitted a listing application form (Form A1) to the Hong Kong Stock Exchange to apply for the listing of, and permission to deal in, the T-Head H Shares on the Main Board.

目前建议以全球发售平头哥 H 股的方式进行，包括：（i）在香港公开发售平头哥 H 股供香港公众认购；（ii）向机构及专业投资者配售平头哥 H 股。

It is currently proposed that the Proposed Spin-off will be effected by way of the Global Offering of T-Head’s H Shares, comprising (i) a Hong Kong public offering of T-Head’s H Shares for subscription by the public in Hong Kong; and (ii) a placing of T-Head’s H Shares to institutional and professional investors.

截至公告日，平头哥为公司全资子公司，是中国领先的全栈数据中心芯片提供商，支撑系统级算力解决方案。建议分拆完成后，预期平头哥仍为公司子公司。

As at the date of this announcement, T-Head is a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company and is a leading provider of full-stack data center chips which enable system-level compute solutions in China. Following completion of the Proposed Spin-off, it is expected that T-Head will remain as a subsidiary of the Company.

建议分拆的详情，包括全球发售的规模与结构、公司于平头哥持股比例的下降幅度，尚未落实确定。

Details in respect of the Proposed Spin-off, including the size and structure of the Global Offering and the extent of the decrease in the Company’s shareholding percentage in T-Head, have not yet been finalized.

二、建议分拆的理由及裨益2. Reasons for and Benefits of the Proposed Spin-off

公司认为建议分拆对阿里与平头哥在商业上均有利，且符合股东整体利益，理由如下：

(a) 使投资者能更好地评估和估值阿里巴巴保留集团的业务。

(a) the Proposed Spin-off should enable investors to better assess and value Alibaba’s Retained Group business.

(b) 能更好地反映平头哥集团自身的价值，投资者可将平头哥的表现与潜力，同保留集团分别、独立地评估。

(b) the Proposed Spin-off could better reflect the value of the T-Head Group on its own merits, and investors would be able to appraise and assess the performance and potential of T-Head separately and distinctly from those of the Retained Group.

(c) 平头哥业务可吸引希望专门投资该业务的投资者，而保留集团继续经营相对更多元化的业务模式。

(c) T-Head’s business may attract investors seeking dedicated exposure to its business, while the Retained Group continues to operate a relatively more diversified business model.

(d) 预期通过建议分拆提升平头哥集团的价值，从而惠及阿里与股东整体。除其他事项外，于香港联交所上市将：(i) 提升平头哥在客户、供应商及潜在战略合作伙伴中的独立形象，有利其洽谈与争取更多业务；(ii) 使平头哥未来在需要时可独立、直接进入股权与债务资本市场，并进一步强化其取得银行授信的能​​力，从而使阿里集团的财务资源得以更有效配置。

(d) the value of the T-Head Group is expected to be enhanced through the Proposed Spin-off which will in turn benefit Alibaba and the Shareholders as a whole, given that a listing on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange will, among other things, (i) enhance T-Head’s standalone profile among its customers, suppliers and potential strategic partners, which will help it to be in a better position to negotiate and solicit more business, and (ii) enable T-Head to directly and independently access both equity and debt capital markets in the future on a standalone basis should the need arise, as well as further enhance its ability to secure bank credit facilities, which allows a more efficient deployment of Alibaba Group’s financial resources.

三、豁免严格遵守 PN15 第 3(f) 段3. Waiver from Strict Compliance with Paragraph 3(f) of PN15

按 PN15 第 3(f) 段，拟分拆的上市公司须顾及其现有股东利益，通过实物分派被分拆实体现有股份、或在任何发行中给予优先认购权，向股东提供被分拆实体股份的保证权利。

Pursuant to paragraph 3(f) of PN15, a listed company contemplating a spin-off is required to have due regard to the interests of its existing Shareholders by providing them with an assured entitlement to the shares of the spun-off entity, either by way of a distribution in specie of existing shares in the spun-off entity or by way of preferred application in any offering of existing or new shares in the spun-off entity.

公司已申请、香港联交所已授予豁免，免于严格遵守向股东提供平头哥 H 股保证权利的规定，依据包括但不限于：

The Company has applied for, and the Hong Kong Stock Exchange has granted, a waiver from strict compliance with the requirement that the Shareholders be provided an assured entitlement to T-Head’s H Shares (the “Waiver”) on the following basis that includes but is not limited to:

(a) 以实物分派或发行中优先认购方式提供保证权利，所需投入与成本相对股东所获利益不成比例或过高——因美国证券法将要求平头哥在美国进行公开发售，从而须向美国 SEC 提交注册声明等，程序繁琐过重；

(a) the extensive endeavor and substantial costs for providing the assured entitlement by way of distribution in specie or preferred application would be disproportionate or excessive to the benefits conferred thereby, as U.S. securities laws would require T-Head to conduct a public offering in the United States and thereby file a registration statement with the SEC under U.S. securities laws, among other procedures, that would be unduly burdensome;

(b) 由于适用于作为中国法下设立的公司之平头哥、以及作为双重上市公司的本公司之中国法律与运营限制（包括但不限于中国法律对全球发售前已发行股份的锁定期要求），实物分派平头哥 H 股将过重且不切实可行；

(b) a distribution in specie of T-Head’s H Shares would be unduly burdensome and impracticable due to the PRC legal and operational restrictions applicable to T-Head as a company established under PRC laws and the Company as a dual listed company, including but not limited to the lock-up on existing shares issued prior to the Global Offering under PRC laws;

(c) 就豁免 PN15 第 3(f) 段所述保证权利寻求股东批准，将过重且在实际操作上并无必要；

(c) it would be unduly burdensome and not practically necessary to seek Shareholders’ approval to waive the assured entitlement as set out in paragraph 3(f) of PN15; and

(d) 参照公司总资产、收入及净利润，建议分拆对公司及股东而言并不重大。

(d) the Proposed Spin-off is insignificant to the Company and its Shareholders with reference to the Company’s total assets, revenue and net income.

董事会认为建议分拆及豁免属公平合理，并符合公司及股东整体利益。

The Board is of the view that the Proposed Spin-off and the Waiver are fair and reasonable and in the interests of the Company and the Shareholders as a whole.

四、上市规则项下的影响4. Implications under the Listing Rules

由于预期建议分拆的最高适用百分比率低于 5%，若建议分拆进行，并不构成上市规则项下的须予披露交易。同时预期若建议分拆落实，不会影响公司在香港联交所与纽交所的双重主要上市地位。

As it is expected that the highest of the applicable percentage ratios in respect of the Proposed Spin-off would be less than 5%, the Proposed Spin-off, if it proceeds, would not constitute a discloseable transaction for the Company under the Listing Rules. It is also expected that the Proposed Spin-off, if materializes, will not affect the Company’s dual primary listing status on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange and the NYSE.

公司将遵守上市规则下的适用规定，并在适当时候就建议分拆作进一步公告。

The Company will comply with the applicable requirements under the Listing Rules and make further announcement(s) in relation to the Proposed Spin-off as and when appropriate.

建议分拆尚须待（其中包括）香港联交所上市委员会批准平头哥 H 股上市及买卖、完成平头哥 H 股上市及发售的中国证监会备案、以及公司及平头哥的最终决定后方可作实。概不保证建议分拆将会进行或何时进行。公司股东及潜在投资者于买卖或投资公司证券时应审慎行事。

The Proposed Spin-off is subject to, among other things, the obtaining of approvals from the Listing Committee of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange for the listing of, and permission to deal in, T-Head’s H Shares, the completion of the filing with the China Securities Regulatory Commission for the listing and offering of T-Head’s H Shares, and the final decisions of the Company and T-Head. There is no assurance that the Proposed Spin-off will take place or as to when it may take place. Shareholders and potential investors of the Company should therefore exercise caution when dealing in or investing in the securities of the Company.
